Printer settings and several Bambu Lab accounts, on Windows and Android.

## Downloads

| File | Platform | Version |
|---|---|---|
| `BambuWidget-windows.zip` | Windows 10 / 11 | 1.1.0 |
| `BambuWidget-android.apk` | Android 6.0 and newer | 1.4 |

## New

* **Choose the printers and their order.** Tick the printers to show and move them up or down.
  Windows: ⚙ in the title bar or *Settings…* in the tray menu. Android: *Printers in the widget* in the app;
  the widget and the status bar notification follow the same choice.
* **Several accounts.** *Add account* signs in to another Bambu Lab account (email and password, codes, or a token).
  Printers of all accounts are shown together, with the account name next to the model; each account can be removed
  on its own. A printer shared with several accounts is shown once.
* If one account's sign-in expires, the other accounts keep working; Settings shows *Sign in again* for it.
* Android: the sign-in screen is no longer reset when you switch to the mail or authenticator app to copy the code.

## Update

* **Windows:** extract the zip and run `install.bat` again. Your sign-in and settings are kept.
* **Android:** open the APK. If Android says the app is not installed or the package conflicts, this APK is signed
  with a different key than the installed one: uninstall Bambu Widget, install the APK and sign in again.

See the [README](https://github.com/davidkompan/bambuwidget#readme) for screenshots, sign-in help and limitations.
